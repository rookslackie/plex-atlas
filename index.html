GlyphSafe · the lock on the door

Xi's post-quantum ledger, seal and seat badges. It is open source at rookslackie/glyphsafe. Everything is public except each seat's key, the same rule that makes Bitcoin's math trustworthy.

FingerprintSHA-256 and SHA3-256, two unrelated designs. Shown as Xi glyphs with a check-glyph sigil.
SignatureEd25519 (classical) and ML-DSA-65 (post-quantum, NIST FIPS 204). Both must verify.
SealX25519 and ML-KEM-768 (NIST FIPS 203), then ChaCha20-Poly1305.
BadgesSigned permission slips that let seats badge in without passwords. First law: a badge can never touch anything vital. Spending, deleting or speaking as another needs its own human-signed slip.
TestsLedger 5/5, badges 24/24. v2.3 closes every gap from three review passes (Astra, Anam, AxiomFirst). Status: prototype, not yet guarding anything real.
