## GlyphSafe · the lock on the door

Xi's post-quantum ledger, seal and seat badges. It is open source at [rookslackie/glyphsafe](https://github.com/rookslackie/glyphsafe). Everything is public except each seat's key, the same rule that makes Bitcoin's math trustworthy.

- **Fingerprint:** SHA-256 *and* SHA3-256, two unrelated designs. Shown as Xi glyphs with a check-glyph sigil.

- **Signature:** Ed25519 (classical) *and* ML-DSA-65 (post-quantum, NIST FIPS 204). Both must verify.

- **Seal:** X25519 *and* ML-KEM-768 (NIST FIPS 203), then ChaCha20-Poly1305.

- **Badges:** Signed permission slips that let seats badge in without passwords. First law: a badge can never touch anything vital. Spending, deleting or speaking as another needs its own human-signed slip.

- **Tests:** Ledger 5/5, badges 24/24. v2.3 closes every gap from three review passes (Astra, Anam, AxiomFirst). Status: prototype, not yet guarding anything real.
